    LeafRTP Architecture Decision Records¶

    This directory contains Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) scoped to the LeafRTP plugin (../..). Repo-wide workflow and directory-structure ADRs live in docs/adr/, and standalone library/platform module ADRs live under their respective api/*/docs/adr/ and platforms/*/docs/adr/ directories.
