# RTP Roadmap

Forward-looking work is tracked in the Linear workspace, team **LeafRTP** (issue IDs `RTP-<n>`).

- Open features, polish, and long-tail items: Linear team `LeafRTP`, backlog.
- Shipped work: [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and the ADRs in [`docs/adr/`](../adr/README.md).
- Intake for incidental findings: [`POTENTIAL_BUGS.md`](POTENTIAL_BUGS.md); filing rules are in `.junie/AGENTS.md` (*Issue Tracking (Linear)*).

New caveats and operator-requested features are filed as Linear issues. Caveats that affect users are mirrored in `docs/publishing/FRONT_PAGE.md` (rebuild with `scripts/release/build_front_pages.py`) as a plain, non-technical summary with no REQ-* or ADR identifiers.
